The SCORM export generates a `.zip` package that you can import in a SCORM-compatible LMS (Moodle, etc) to offer your ePoc to your learners.

## Export

::steps{level=5}
##### Click the arrow on the right of the **Publish** button in the top right
##### Choose **Export SCORM**
##### Choose a location and a name for the `.zip` file
::

The package contains `index.html` and `imsmanifest.xml` at the root of the archive, along with the ePoc player and your ePoc content in `assets/`. The manifest is automatically generated from your ePoc identifier and title.

## Import in an LMS

Add a **SCORM** activity to your course and upload the exported `.zip` file without extracting it. The exact steps depend on your LMS, refer to its documentation.

::alert{icon="lucide:info"}
The package uses SCORM 1.2. When the LMS allows it, the learner's progress and score are reported to it.
::

## Which export to choose?

| Need | Export |
| --- | --- |
| Share the ePoc in the mobile application | [Publish](/en/guide/user/export-and-publish) (`.epoc`) |
| Put the ePoc online in a browser | [HTML export](/en/guide/user/export-and-publish/html) |
| Integrate the ePoc in an LMS | SCORM export |
