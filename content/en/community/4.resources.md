ePoc is a free project: beyond the official documentation, users create and share their own resources to help others get started with the solution.

This page gathers these contributions: video tutorials, articles, training materials, etc.

::alert{icon="lucide:info"}
These resources are created and maintained by their authors, independently of the ePoc team.
::

## Video tutorials

- [Tutorial about ePoc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ef17MSBbYhU) (in French) by Johann Nallet, history-geography teacher and digital education trainer.

## Tools

- [ePoc prompt generator](https://laurentbernard.forge.apps.education.fr/Prompt-ePoc/) (in French) by Laurent Bernard: generates a prompt to create an ePoc with an AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, etc.).
- [Papillote](https://papillote.forge.apps.education.fr/) (in French) by Emmanuel Gaunard, project manager at the DRANE: a website to design an ePoc with the help of an AI (ILAAS by default or the one of your choice), based on the French school curricula. It lets you download the `.epoc` file, share it with students via a QR code, save it to Nuage or export it as SCORM. See the [presentation video](https://podeduc.apps.education.fr/video/164974-papillote-creation-depoc-assistee-par-ia/) and the [documentation](https://papillote.forge.apps.education.fr/decouvrir/).

## ePocs

- [ePocs about cybersecurity](https://forge.apps.education.fr/cybersecurite/epoc) by Charles Poulmaire: two courses available in French and English, with badges and a certificate.
  - **Good practices**: an introductory course in 4 chapters (securing access, protecting personal data, communications, devices), featuring videos from [Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/).
  - **OSINT**: a thematic course in 3 chapters to get started with open-source intelligence (definition, hands-on practice, ethical questions).
